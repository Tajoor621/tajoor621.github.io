PhoneKey

A free WireGuard client for Android. No ads, no subscription, no account, and no tracking. You bring your own server. Xray and SSTP are not implemented.

The website is for information and download. A tunnel starts in the installed app, after Android's permission prompt, using a WireGuard profile from your own server.

Android 8.0 or newer.
